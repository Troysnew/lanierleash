Privacy

Short and plain

Last updated November 20, 2026

What we collect

If you sign up for the weekend email: your email address, your ZIP code and the distance you picked. We use the ZIP's rough center point to work out which events are near you. That's it. No name, no exact address, no tracking pixels in our emails.

If you change your ZIP or distance on the site without signing up, that's saved only in your own browser.

How we use it

Only to send the Thursday weekend email and the confirm and welcome notes. We never sell, rent or share your address. We don't run ads that use your data.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the subscriber list.
  • Resend delivers the emails.
  • Vercel hosts the site.

The forecast comes from Open-Meteo and maps from OpenStreetMap. Your browser talks to them directly to show weather and maps; we don't send them anything about you.

Leaving

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted entirely, reply to any email or write to hello@lanierleash.com and we'll remove it.

Listings

Lanier Leash is an independent local guide. We aren't affiliated with the venues, parks or agencies we list. Dog rules change, so check with the place before you go.

Privacy

Short and plain

Last updated November 21, 2026

What we collect

If you sign up for the Thursday email: your email address, your ZIP code and the distance you picked. We use the ZIP's rough center point to work out which events are near you. That's it. No name, no exact address, no tracking pixels in our emails.

If you change your ZIP or distance on the site without signing up, that's saved only in your own browser.

If you tap Use my location, your browser asks first. Your approximate position (rounded to about 30 feet) stays in your browser to work out distances, and is sent once to OpenStreetMap to look up your ZIP code. We don't receive or store it.

If you vote on a place (thumbs up or down), we store your vote and the tags you picked, with a scrambled code made from your internet address so each person gets one vote per place. We never store the address itself.

If you post a lost or found pet, we store what you put in the post, your photo, your email and, if you give it, your phone number. The post, photo and your first name are public; your phone is shown only if you tick the box; your email is never shown. When someone messages you through a post, we email their message to you and keep only a record that a message was sent (no text), to stop spam. Taking your post down deletes the photo.

On featured partner pages and cards, we count page views and taps on buttons like Call or Directions so we can tell partners how their listing is doing. Each count uses a scrambled code that changes every day, so we can tell one person from another for that day only and can't follow anyone over time. Partners only ever see totals.

If you send us something with the submit form (an event, a place, a correction or your pup), we keep what you typed, your email so we can reply, and the same kind of scrambled code to slow down spam. If we pick your pup, their photo, name, age, town, the line you wrote and your first name (if you gave it) go on the site and in the email. Your email is never shown.

How we use it

To send the Thursday email and the confirm and welcome notes, pass lost & found messages along, count votes and partner taps, and review what you send us. We never sell or rent your email, and we don't share it with sponsors or partners. Sponsors appear in the email, but they never get our list. We don't run ads that use your data.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores our data and photos.
  • Resend delivers the emails, including lost & found messages.
  • Vercel hosts the site.

The forecast comes from Open-Meteo, maps from OpenStreetMap, fonts from Google Fonts and the map code from cdnjs. Your browser loads these directly, so like any website they see your internet address. We don't send them your email or anything you've told us (the one exception is the Use my location lookup above, which you start).

Leaving

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your email deleted entirely, reply to any email or write to hello@lanierleash.com and we'll remove it.

Listings

Lanier Leash is an independent local guide. We aren't affiliated with the venues, parks or agencies we list. Dog rules change, so check with the place before you go.

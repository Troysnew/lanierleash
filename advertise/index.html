Reach local dog owners every Thursday

One sponsor per email, clearly labeled, in front of dog owners around Cumming and Lake Lanier who are planning their weekend.

What you get

  • The sponsor spot in the Thursday email. Your name, one or two lines and a link, right after the weekend's events. Only one sponsor per issue.
  • Readers who are making plans. The email goes out Thursday morning, when people are deciding where to go this weekend with their dog.
  • Local only. Readers sign up with their ZIP code, and almost all of them live around Forsyth, Hall, Dawson, north Gwinnett and north Fulton.

Founding sponsors

We're new. The first local businesses to sponsor get their first three issues free, so you can see how it works before you pay anything. After that it's a simple flat price per issue, based on how many people get the email. Ask and we'll send current numbers, or read this week's email to see exactly what readers get.

The rules

Sponsors are always labeled "Sponsor." Sponsoring never changes a dog rule, a badge or whether a place is listed. Businesses that aren't dog-friendly can't sponsor. A free listing is open to every dog-friendly business: claim yours.

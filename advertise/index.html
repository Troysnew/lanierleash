Reach local dog owners every Thursday

One sponsor per email, clearly labeled, in front of dog owners around Cumming and Lake Lanier who are planning their weekend.

What you get

  • The sponsor spot in the Thursday email. Your name, one or two lines and a link, right after the weekend's events. Only one sponsor per issue.
  • Readers who are making plans. The email goes out Thursday morning, when people are deciding where to go this weekend with their dog.
  • Local only. Readers sign up with their ZIP code, and almost all of them live around Forsyth, Hall, Dawson, north Gwinnett and north Fulton.

Founding sponsors

We're new. The first local businesses to sponsor get their first three issues free, so you can see how it works before you pay anything. Read this week's email to see exactly what readers get.

Pricing

A flat price per Thursday email, set by how many people get it. The price goes up only when the list grows into the next tier, and we tell you before it does. Ask and we'll send this week's numbers.

Featured partner listings

For vets, groomers, trainers, daycares, pet stores and dog-friendly restaurants, breweries and stays.

  • Your own page on Lanier Leash. Photos, services, hours, call and booking buttons, and an offer just for our readers.
  • A labeled card at the top of the right page. Vet care for vets, dog services for groomers, trainers and daycares, and your category in places for everyone else.
  • A mention in the first Thursday email of every month.
  • A monthly report. How many people saw your page and tapped call, directions, booking or your offer.
  • Two partners per category at most, so your spot stays worth having.

$79 a month, or $790 a year (two months free). Founding partners get their first month free. Cancel anytime; your page comes down at the end of the month you've paid for. We send a simple invoice or payment link.

The rules

Sponsors and partners are always labeled "Sponsored." Paying never changes a dog rule, a badge, reader reports or whether a place is listed, and the emergency vet list is never sponsored or reordered. Vets must be licensed. Businesses that aren't dog-friendly can't sponsor. A free listing is open to every dog-friendly business: claim yours.

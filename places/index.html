What the badges mean

Dog rules change. Every place shows where its rule came from and the date we last looked, so you can judge for yourself.

Distances are straight-line miles from your ZIP, not driving distance. Photos on this page are illustrations, not pictures of the places. Lake Lanier parks are run by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and counties; swim beaches often have different pet rules than the rest of the park.

Choosing a groomer or daycare

  • Ask to see where dogs wait and play. Clean, cool and supervised is what you want.
  • Ask which vaccines they require. Good places ask for rabies, DHPP and Bordetella.
  • For daycare, ask about a trial day and how they group dogs by size and energy.

Choosing a trainer

  • Look for reward-based methods. Ask how they handle a dog who gets it wrong.
  • Certifications like CPDT-KA mean they passed an independent exam.
  • Watch a class first if you can. Dogs and people should both look relaxed.

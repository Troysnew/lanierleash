Affordable vet care near Lake Lanier

Nonprofit clinics, low-cost spay and neuter, vaccine clinics, pet food pantries and help with vet bills around Cumming, Gainesville and north metro Atlanta.

Low-cost vet clinics

Humane Society of Forsyth County - Affordable Pet Wellness Clinic

Cumming
Services
Wellness exams, vaccines, microchips, heartworm tests and treatment, spay/neuter
Cost
Wellness exam $35, DHPP $25, 1-year rabies $30, microchip $30 (current price list)
Who can use it
Open to everyone; appointment required

Clinic hours Thursday through Saturday mornings; no proof of income needed.

4440 Keith Bridge Road, Cumming, GA 30041

770-887-6480

Gainesville
Services
Annual vaccines, heartworm testing, discounted prevention, spay/neuter
Who can use it
Appointment only

Basic wellness care, not a full-service clinic; book online.

845 West Ridge Road, Gainesville, GA 30501

Spay and neuter

Atlanta Humane Society - Low-Cost Spay/Neuter

Atlanta
Services
Spay/neuter with vaccines and microchip included
Cost
Dogs from $225 by weight (current AHS price list)
Who can use it
Some pets excluded, such as pets 7 or older and certain flat-faced breeds

By appointment, up to four weeks ahead; surgeries are done at the Atlanta location.

404-875-5331

Website

Dawson County Humane Society - Free Spay/Neuter Program

Dawsonville
Services
Spay/neuter
Cost
Free for qualifying pets
Who can use it
Dawson County residents who are DFCS clients or active military families; other low-income residents can call to ask

Call and ask for the free spay/neuter program to book.

633 Martin Rd., Dawsonville, GA 30534

(706) 216-8387

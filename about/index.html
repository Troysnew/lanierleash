About

A local guide for dog people

Lanier Leash is a free guide to dog-friendly events and places around Cumming, Lake Lanier and north metro Atlanta. It's run by one guy and one very good boy.

Meet the pack

Hi, I'm Troy. That's Riley.

I live in Cumming with my golden retriever, Riley. If he looks familiar, it's because he's the guy smiling on our home page. Yes, that's really him, and no, he has not stopped bringing it up.

Riley handles field testing: patios, trails, lake parks and any place that might drop a pizza crust. I handle the boring part, like reading the actual dog rules, checking the dates and writing the Thursday email.

Between the two of us, we just want it to be easy to find where your dog is welcome around the lake. If we got something wrong, or you know a spot we'd love, tell us. Riley takes recommendations very seriously.

  • Job title: Co-founder (unpaid, works for pizza crust)
  • Specialties: shaking off right next to you, befriending strangers, carrying sticks too big for the trail
  • Rating system: tail wags. Everything so far has been a 10.

What we do

Every Thursday morning we send a short email with the weekend's dog-friendly events near your ZIP, a walk check with the forecast and a pavement estimate, and a place worth trying. The website has the full events calendar and a directory of restaurants, breweries, trails, parks, lake access, pet stores and hotels that welcome dogs, plus groomers, trainers, vet care, adoption and a lost & found board.

How we check

Dog rules change, and a lot of "dog-friendly" lists online are out of date. So every place shows its dog rule, where that rule came from, and the date we last checked. "Official rule" means the place or the agency that runs it posts the rule. "Venue confirmed" means they told us directly. "Unconfirmed" means we only found it on a third-party site, so call ahead. Independent

We aren't affiliated with any venue, county or agency. Sponsors are always labeled, and they never change a dog rule or a badge. How sponsorship works.

Help us keep it right

Readers make this better. Send an event, suggest a place, fix a listing, or send your pup for Pup of the Week. Own a dog-friendly business? Claim your listing for free. You can also reply to any of our emails.

Your privacy

We use your email to send the Thursday email and anything you've asked us for, and you can unsubscribe in one click. We don't sell it or share it with sponsors. Privacy details.

About

A local guide for dog people

Lanier Leash is a free guide to dog-friendly events and places around Cumming, Lake Lanier and north metro Atlanta.

What we do

Every Thursday morning we send a short email with the weekend's dog-friendly events near your ZIP, a walk check with the forecast and a pavement estimate, and a few places worth trying. The website has the full events calendar and a directory of restaurants, breweries, trails, parks, lake access, pet stores and hotels that welcome dogs.

How we check

Dog rules change, and a lot of "dog-friendly" lists online are out of date. So every place shows its dog rule, where that rule came from, and the date we last checked. A green badge means the place or the agency that runs it says so. "Unconfirmed" means we only found it on a third-party site, so call ahead. More on the badges.

Independent

We aren't affiliated with any venue, county or agency. Sponsors are always labeled, and they never change a dog rule or a badge. How sponsorship works.

Help us keep it right

Readers make this better. Send an event, suggest a place, fix a listing, or send your pup for Pup of the Week. Run a dog-friendly business? Claim your listing for free. You can also reply to any of our emails.

Your privacy

We only use your email to send the weekly guide, and you can unsubscribe in one click. We don't sell or share it. Privacy details.

Lost your dog? The first hour

  1. Search close and call their name calmly. Most dogs are found within a mile. Bring treats and a squeaky toy; leave a worn shirt and water by your door.
  2. Post here and share it in neighborhood Facebook groups and Nextdoor. Print the flyer from your post page for mailboxes and vet offices.
  3. Call the county shelter and nearby vets, and visit in person every couple of days. Shelters and rescues near you.
  4. Make sure the microchip is registered to your current phone number.

Found a dog?

  1. Check for tags, then have any vet or shelter scan for a microchip. It's free.
  2. Post here with a photo and where you found them. Leave out one detail, like a marking, so you can check the real owner.
  3. File a found report with the county shelter. Owners look there first.
  4. Hurt or sick? Emergency vets open now.
